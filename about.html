The Daily Bean Story.

Founded in 2003, Brewing Excellence & Giving Back

The Daily Bean was founded in 2003, the same year our current CEO Owethu Jezile was born. What started as a passion for quality coffee has grown into a thriving community space where every cup tells a story. We're proud to dedicate 20% of our profits to educate and provide support for the less fortunate in our community, because we believe great coffee should make a positive impact beyond just great taste.
